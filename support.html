It helps to include your device (for example "iPhone 15" or "iPad Air"), the in-game day you're on, and what happened.

Common questions

Where is my game saved?

On your device. The game saves on its own, including when you leave the app. Deleting the app deletes your saves.

How do I play with touch?

Drag anywhere on the left side of the screen to walk with the joystick. The green button on the right does the main action (talk, plant, water, serve), and the Pick Up and Rotate buttons appear when there's something to pick up or turn.

I bought the full game on another device. How do I get it here?

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID.

The game feels slow on my device.

Try closing other apps. If it's still slow, send us your device model so we can make it run better.
