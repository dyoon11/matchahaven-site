About the Game

You've just arrived on a little island with a seed, a watering can and a dream: the best matcha in the world.

Plant tea and care for it day by day. Grind the leaves into matcha powder, then mix it with milks, sweeteners, foam, boba and fruit from every corner of the island to make over 100 different drinks.

Serve the villagers who line up at your counter, earn coins, and upgrade Matcha Haven from a tiny shop into a matcha palace. Decorate it your way, build a home, adopt a dog or cat, and explore new lands when you're ready for an adventure.
