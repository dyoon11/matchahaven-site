Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Short version: Matcha Haven does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game collects

Nothing. Matcha Haven has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send any information over the internet.

Your saved game

Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

If the game offers an in-app purchase, it is handled entirely by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. See Apple's privacy policy for how they handle purchases.

Our website

If you send us a message or join our email list through the Contact Us form at matchahavengame.com, we receive the name and email address you enter and use them only to reply to you and to send the updates you asked for. You can ask to be removed at any time. That website uses cookies to count visits, as its cookie notice explains.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Reach us through the Support page.
